# Module 8. Integrated Regional Application and Policy Evaluation

Draft teaching plan for instructor review.

When does a modeled increase in regional activity support a policy recommendation? Students compare a policy with an explicit counterfactual and identify whose gains, costs, and external effects enter the evaluation (LO7, drawing on LO6).

## Teaching sequence

- **Dec 16, after M7.** Return to the PS 4 scenario. Define the affected region, residents, and alternative use of resources. Separate gross output, income, and welfare, and identify one group that could lose even when regional output rises.
- **Dec 17.** Compare the IO and CGE explanations for the same intervention. Identify displacement, trade leakages, and an external effect that would need separate evidence. Use the remaining time for final-exam review.

## Readings and teaching materials

Hoover and Giarratani, §12.2, pp. 250–255, frames regional objectives. §12.5, pp. 263–267, supports discussion of strategy. The CSU introductory workshop, PDF pp. 64–68 and 81–95, provides a food-system example with displaced purchases and opportunity costs.

## Assessment connection

Use the bounded policy interpretation in PS 4, due Dec 16. For the Dec 18 final, students can interpret supplied results, state the counterfactual, and identify evidence needed for a welfare claim.
