# Module 3. Defining and Measuring a Regional Economy

Draft teaching plan for instructor review.

Which regional boundary and benchmark make a claim about specialization meaningful? Students build a regional profile in R and explain how the choice of comparison affects it (LO5, building on LO3).

## Teaching sequence

- **Nov 26, after M2.** Use the M2 corridor to compare an administrative region with a commuting area. Before combining employment tables, check boundaries, years, sector definitions, and units.
- **Nov 27, before M4.** Calculate one location quotient by hand, then compare sectors in R. Decompose employment change into benchmark growth, industry mix, and regional shift. Ask what else could explain a positive regional shift.

## Readings and teaching materials

Schaffer, ch. 2, pp. 6–9, motivates boundary choice. Note A, pp. 43–47, supplies the measurement techniques. For a second treatment of functional regions and specialization, use Hoover and Giarratani, §§9.1–9.4 and appendix 12-1.

## Assessment connection

Use the PS 1 checkpoint on Nov 27 for feedback before the regional profile is due Nov 30. Exam 1 can ask students to interpret a quotient or a growth component.
